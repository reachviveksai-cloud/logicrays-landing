01 · The shift

What is agentic commerce?

Agentic commerce is shopping done by AI agents on a person's behalf. Instead of browsing ten stores, a shopper asks their AI assistant — and the assistant finds products, compares stores, checks stock, delivery and return terms, and completes the purchase.

For your store, that means the visitor deciding whether to buy is increasingly a piece of software acting for a human. Software doesn't scroll, doesn't squint at product photos, and doesn't give the benefit of the doubt. It reads what your store publishes — and if it can't read something, for the agent that thing doesn't exist.

02 · Why SEO isn't enough

Being mentioned by AI is marketing. Being buyable by AI is revenue.

Search asked one question: who ranks? AI shopping asks a harder one: who can I buy from — right now, with confidence? SEO and its new coat of paint, AEO, solve the first question. They get your store talked about. Agentic readiness solves the second: it makes your store the one the agent can actually complete a purchase from — verified identity, quotable return and shipping terms, a checkout an agent can drive.

03 · What "ready" actually means

Getting AI-ready isn't one switch. It's five layers.

Every layer is a question an AI agent silently asks about your store. Answer all five and agents can find you, trust you and buy from you. Fail one and everything above it stops mattering — a store that can't be trusted doesn't get to be bought from.

1Can AI find your store?

Crawler permissions, sitemaps, secure connection — the front door. Most stores pass, often by accident.

2Can it read your products?

Prices, stock, images and descriptions in machine-readable form — not just pixels a human can see.

3Can it trust who you are?

Verified store identity that an agent can check cryptographically — the difference between a store and a storefront-shaped page.

4Can it actually buy?

Return and shipping terms an agent can quote before purchasing, payment methods it can use, a checkout it can complete.

5Will it still work next month?

Standards are evolving monthly. Monitoring and re-checks keep a ready store ready.

SEO and AEO end at layer 2. Layers 3–5 are where sales happen — and where we work.

Are AI agents really buying from online stores today?

The AI crawlers are already on your store — GPTBot, PerplexityBot and Google's AI crawlers visit ecommerce sites today, and AI assistants already recommend specific products and stores. Completed agent checkouts are earlier, but the stores that are readable and trustworthy now are the ones agents learn to recommend first. We already invest in SEO — isn't that enough?

SEO and answer-engine optimisation get your store mentioned by AI. Agentic readiness gets your store bought from. Being mentioned is layers one and two of a five-layer problem; an agent that finds you but cannot verify your identity, read your return terms or complete your checkout recommends a competitor it can. Most stores we audit are strong on visibility and empty on buyability.

Is this just adding schema markup to my site?

Markup is one layer of five. Full readiness also covers verified store identity, machine-readable return and shipping terms an agent can quote before buying, a checkout an agent can complete, and monitoring so it all keeps working.
