01 · The shift

What is agentic commerce?

Agentic commerce is shopping done by AI agents on a person's behalf. Instead of browsing ten stores, a shopper asks their AI assistant — and it finds products, compares stores, checks stock, delivery and return terms, and completes the purchase.

For your store, the visitor deciding whether to buy is increasingly a piece of software. Software doesn't scroll, squint at photos, or give the benefit of the doubt. It reads what your store publishes. If it can't read something, for the agent that thing doesn't exist.

02 · Why SEO isn't enough

Being mentioned by AI is marketing. Being buyable by AI is revenue.

Search asked one question: who ranks? AI shopping asks a harder one: who can I buy from, right now, with confidence? SEO and its new coat of paint, AEO, answer the first. They get your store talked about. Agentic readiness answers the second: it makes your store the one an agent can actually complete a purchase from — verified identity, quotable return and shipping terms, a checkout it can drive.

03 · What "ready" actually means

Getting AI-ready isn't one switch. It's five layers.

Every layer is a question an AI agent silently asks about your store. Answer all five and agents can find you, trust you and buy from you. Fail one and everything above it stops mattering — a store that can't be trusted doesn't get to be bought from.

1
Can AI find your store?

Crawler permissions, sitemaps, a secure connection — the front door. Most stores pass, often by accident.

2
Can it read your products?

Prices, stock, images and descriptions in machine-readable form, not just pixels a human can see.

▼  SEO and AEO stop here. Below this line is where the sale happens.
3
Can it trust who you are?

A verified store identity an agent can check — the difference between a store and a storefront-shaped page.

4
Can it actually buy?

Return and shipping terms an agent can quote before purchasing, payment it can use, a checkout it can complete.

5
Will it still work next month?

Standards are evolving monthly. Monitoring and re-checks keep a ready store ready.

07 · Questions merchants ask

The honest answers, before you book.

What is agentic commerce?

Agentic commerce is shopping done by AI agents on a person's behalf. Instead of browsing stores, a shopper asks an AI assistant, and it finds products, compares stores, checks stock, delivery and return terms, and completes the purchase. For a merchant, the visitor deciding whether to buy is increasingly a piece of software acting for a human.

Are AI agents really buying from online stores today?

The AI crawlers are already on your store. GPTBot, PerplexityBot and Google's AI crawlers visit ecommerce sites today, and AI assistants already recommend specific products and stores. The stores that are readable and trustworthy now are the ones agents learn to recommend first. The audit is free, so the cost of knowing where you stand is zero.

We already invest in SEO. Isn't that enough?

SEO and answer-engine optimisation get your store mentioned by AI. Agentic readiness gets your store bought from. Being mentioned is the first two layers of a five-layer problem; an agent that finds you but cannot verify your identity, read your return terms or complete your checkout recommends a competitor it can. Most stores we audit are strong on visibility and empty on buyability.

Do I need to rebuild my store or change platforms?

No. The work happens on your existing Magento store: the Goofre™ storefront module, configuration, and targeted fixes from the audit's gap list. Your theme, catalog and checkout stay yours. A typical readiness engagement is a fixed-scope project measured in weeks, not a re-platform measured in quarters.

We audit your live store across five layers and hand you a graded A to F report: every gap named in plain English, with its fix and who owns it. What happens next is your call. Many merchants take the report and fix things internally; others engage us for a fixed-scope readiness project. There is no obligation either way.
