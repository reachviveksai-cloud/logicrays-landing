The shift

Search didn't just change. Buying did.

Before

The human storefront

A person searches Google, clicks a link, browses your store, and checks out. Everything you've optimized for a decade assumes a human is looking.

Now

The agent in the loop

A shopper asks an AI. The agent searches, compares, verifies you're real, and increasingly completes the purchase — on stores it can machine-read and trust. Your website isn't in that loop unless the layer underneath it is built for machines.

The uncomfortable part

AEO wins the mention.
Agentic readiness wins the transaction.

Most "AI-ready" advice stops at getting mentioned in the answer. But the agent's next move is to act. It tries to verify you're a safe, real store and complete the purchase. If it can't, it quietly moves to a competitor it can. There's no bounce report for a sale an AI abandoned on your behalf — you just don't get it.

Straight answers

The questions merchants actually ask.

We already do SEO / AEO. Isn't this the same thing?

SEO and AEO are discovery — layers 1–2. They get you mentioned. They do nothing for whether an agent can verify you and check out, which is layers 3–5, where the revenue is. We complement your SEO; we don't replace it.

Is anyone actually shopping through AI yet?

Discovery already moved — buyers research inside AI every day. Transaction is moving now, which is the cheap moment to get ready. The merchants who wait until it's obvious will scramble, at a premium, to do what you can do calmly and first.

Can't my Shopify / Magento just handle this?

Platforms automate the human storefront brilliantly. The machine-readable, verifiable, transactable layer is not native or complete — and platform updates actively break it. Native tools don't monitor that. We do.

Why is this ongoing and not a one-time fix?

Because the target moves. Protocols version, new AI platforms launch with new rules, and your own site changes. "Ready" decays. We track your score over time and catch regressions before they cost you — the same reason SEO was never a one-time job.

How will I know it's working?

Three ways: your readiness score trajectory over time, AI-referral traffic attributed in your own analytics, and crawl logs showing AI agents actually reaching your new endpoints. We report all three monthly. No vanity metrics.

I don't have engineering bandwidth for another project.

You don't need it. Much of the readiness layer we provision for you directly. The rest is a short, prioritized list — handled or handed over with clear ownership. You get a plan, not homework.
